    Animation assignments

    Drop clips here. Death accepts multiple variants.

    Fallback rules: Unassigned actions use 1H Swing, except Idle and Move. If 1H Swing is also missing, the model uses T-pose to indicate the missing animation. Idle and Move require their own assignments. Assigning 1H Swing is a required export checklist item.

    Fit equipment

    Choose an attachment, then move, rotate or resize it. Back fits are saved per model and item. Hand fits can also be reused with other weapons.

    Catalog grips are used until you save a hand fit.

    Quiver arrows

    Lower width makes stored arrows narrower without shortening them. Lower spacing packs them closer. Ammunition limits the visible count. Drawn arrows return to their in-game width.

    Choose a Quiver or Automatic bow quiver to see the pickup point.
