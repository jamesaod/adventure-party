What the controls affect

Roughness spreads highlights. Metalness changes the balance of colored reflection and diffuse light. Shield specular controls the highlight alone. Diffuse compression preserves detail when reflected light becomes intense. Snow reflectance controls how much nearby lamp light the snow reflects.

This isolated outdoor scene uses the production solar model and direct-unit/snow renderer. It excludes battlefield walls, tactical visibility and cast shadows. Clear-noon sky calibration is unchanged. The lamp uses the same inverse-square model lighting as combat.
