# Standalone giant fall / chain-push experiment

Open http://127.0.0.1:8276/corpse-landing-test.html. This is now a standalone Three.js arena, like ragdoll-test.html. It has no iframe, combat session, AI, visibility, blood, damage, or production movement hooks. The previous experimental combat hooks have been removed.

Default: one real Hill Giant and three real Orcs, framed together before Drop giant. Replay resets the actors. Controls: pause/resume, reset, reframe, orbit/zoom, normal/half/quarter playback, optional planned hexes, dust, and optional Orc recovery. Clear, single, chain, open-space preference and blocked-exit fixtures are available. A blocked plan refuses to drop rather than forcing overlap.

Owners: corpse-landing-arena.mjs (scene and playback), corpse-landing-planner.js (pure bounded axial planner), ragdoll-lab-rig.mjs (shared explosion-test rigs). The rig loader now accepts optional unitId and heightMetres; default Orc behavior is unchanged. The giant animation selector retains retargeted UAL1 Death02 and offers the actual Workshop-assigned death (currently Two Handed Sword Death, 2.4 seconds). Assigned frames are sampled through the Workshop animation selector, preserving trim/speed/grounding. Orcs retain Death02. Each giant animation has a separate cached footprint. Optional recovery uses Crawl Exit with the existing explosion-lab blends, not reversed death playback. Giant height in the lab is 16 feet, matching the current authored unit. The footprint is rasterized from the actual final skinned body mesh projected onto the ground, including both legs and arms. Held equipment is excluded. A 4.5 cm sample grid unions triangle coverage; a hex blocks only with at least 0.08 square metres of total body coverage and 0.03 square metres inside a 0.38-metre radius around its center. This gameplay clearance approximation keeps toe-edge slivers and the Death02 between-leg gap open. The pose footprint is cached once and rotated onto candidate fall directions. Empty bounding-box/spine cells are not filled. Contact timing and planner footprint remain experimental; no live combat adoption until reviewed.

Planner checks clear space first, reserves all destinations, rejects cyclic pushes and obstacle crossings, and caps search at 1800 attempts / 12 pushed units / 6 chain levels. No runtime physics simulation. The animation experiment follows the planned one-cell push distances with staggered contact clocks.

Validation: pure planner fixtures and combat regressions (193/193) pass. Headless browser loaded four real actors, replayed a three-Orc chain to settled poses, and screenshots were inspected internally to confirm actors remained framed. Visual tuning remains user review.

Default setup: six Orcs in the six adjacent hexes around the standing giant, facing inward. The planner evaluates all six fall directions. Existing chain, single, clear, crowded, and blocked setups remain selectable. Reframe includes both ground axes and planned push destinations.

Two-ring setup is now the default: 18 Orcs (6 at radius one, 12 at radius two), facing inward. The assigned death pose has an explicit authored blocked-cell addition at canonical axial (1,-1), the arm-side pocket marked by the user. This rotates with its footprint; Death02 clearance exceptions remain unchanged.

Scale correction: combat HEX_W is 64 and repository-mob-frame.mjs calibrates body height at 64 pixels per 10 feet. The lab now uses 3.048 m between hex centers, replacing its erroneous 1.524 m. Models use published height and precise rest-body mesh bounds (Hill Giant 4.8768 m, Orc 1.8288 m), not the explosion lab bone-height estimate. Explosion defaults remain unchanged. Earlier footprint/push counts used the wrong grid and are superseded. At corrected scale Death02 fits without reaching adjacent standing centers; the marked arm-side region is remapped from its old physical position into the corrected grid rather than retaining an obsolete axial index. A standing unit at an adjacent center may now be clear of the actual corpse; previous chain counts are not promised.

Fall angle control: original, 30 degrees clockwise (default), or 30 degrees counterclockwise. Rotates the real giant rig and resamples the final mesh; footprint cache includes animation and angle. The authored arm-side region rotates in physical space before hex quantization. Model scale and Orc placements are unchanged.

## Adopted giant landing rule

Combat now treats huge/gargantuan/giant categories, or effective heights of at least 12 feet, as in-place giant corpses. sampleCorpseOffset returns zero before examining other corpses. Death yaw is exactly +30 or -30 degrees. No random lateral search or displacement of existing bodies. Smaller deaths keep their existing placement behavior.

The Workshop death path applies giant-corpse-settle.mjs during the last 35 percent of the clip: hips/torso and limb bones relax toward the ground plane with bounded rotations and preserved bone lengths. Existing body-floor grounding and terrain slope fitting then apply. It is an articulated approximation, not rigid-body physics or mesh flattening. The existing settled-corpse capture caches the result. The standalone giant rig uses the same settling function. Older corpses under the giant radius receive draw-before ordering, with wall constraints retained. New encounters clear corpseDropInPlace.

Validation: 195/195 combat regression scripts pass, including giant policy, no placement search, length-preserving bone rotation, and older-corpse cover order with unchanged positions.

Standalone contact refinement: Death02 uses head-weighted skinned vertices as its final grounding support, deliberately allowing belly clipping. Assigned death uses a back profile that levels the shoulder line around the torso axis, then adjusts limb joints; torso-weighted body vertices define ground support. Both blend over the final 35% of their clips. In the browser, face support ended at 0.004 m with belly below the floor; back support and body minimum ended at 0.004 m. Production uses the previous default articulated settling until these distinct pose profiles are accepted. Horizontal offsets and hex scale are untouched by this pass.

Visual adjustment pass: Death02 shifts 0.9144 m (3 feet) toward the feet over the final settling interval, rotated with the selected fall angle. This is a standalone presentation offset; the tactical footprint is retained. The back profile lowers the thighs about 14 degrees below horizontal and permits up to 90 degrees of leg correction, reducing the raised-knee support. Default production settling and the explosion test are unchanged. Browser verification confirmed the 3-foot Death02 shift, no shift for assigned death, and preserved face/back ground contact.


### Volumetric impact dust experiment
The standalone arena uses giant-impact-dust.mjs: sixteen bounded ray-marched volumes (24 samples), emitted once at 78% of either death clip from the posed body raster boundary. Opaque scene depth clips dust against actors and ground. Clouds move outward, rise, and expire after three seconds; reset clears them. Daylight, dusk and firelight controls adjust scene and dust illumination. This is approximate lighting, not volumetric shadow transport. An additional full-resolution depth scene pass runs only while dust is active; CPU submission timing is diagnostic, not GPU cost. No production combat integration yet. Heavy Body Fall can be selected with the local audio file picker; Pixabay automated download returned 403, so no audio was bundled. Existing regression suite: 195/195 passed.

The facedown landing offset now eases across the entire death animation (normalized clip time), rather than only its final 35%. Final distance and direction are unchanged; vertical grounding and pose settling retain their separate timing.

User supplied Universfield Heavy Body Fall MP3 is now bundled unchanged under assets/audio/sfx/hill-giant, with source metadata. The standalone arena preloads it automatically and plays it at the existing giant impact cue; optional local replacement remains available.
