# Treebreaker Orc — local combat test

Choose **Treebreaker Orc (special · 12 ft · 65 HP)** in Combat Setup’s MOB type dropdown. Each selected special unit uses the existing Orc Warrior model with 144 inches (12 feet), 65 current/max HP. Ordinary Orc Warrior selections are never replaced. Count and faction controls apply; the special unit remains AI-controlled even when friendly. Use the grass test for trees. The published Workshop template stays unchanged.

AI priority is exclusive until armed: find a reachable standing specimen with a clear fall corridor; walk adjacent using normal movement; face its trunk; play UAL1 `Push_Enter`; fell it in the exact direction from the orc toward the tree; play `Push_Exit`; wait until debris settles; path beside the trunk midpoint; play `PickUp_Kneeling`; attach the original center-trunk geometry as a hand-held club. The supplied UAL1 source GLB contains all three clips. They use the existing humanoid retargeter and shared combat presentation scheduler. Normal combat AI resumes on a later turn after pickup. Weapon selection and reaction attacks cannot bypass the objective. Existing movement reactions and environmental hazards still affect him.

The forced fall direction never substitutes another direction. Other living units retain their fall-corridor protection. If no valid tree/path is available, the orc waits and retries on its next turn. Movement uses the normal per-turn movement limit. Pushing and picking up each finish their current AI turn.

Pickup removes only the center trunk from tree rendering and obstacle capsules; stump, branches and leaves remain. The carried object shares the source shape but owns cloned geometry/materials/textures for unit rendering. Its visibility and material binding are independent of tree mask passes. Held-log body rendering has a larger crop. Tree reset removes held logs and restarts this objective; encounter replacement/death interrupts actions.

Weapon damage is initially the ordinary club baseline: 1d6 against small/medium and 1d3 against large targets, bludgeoning. No invented giant strength bonus or increased reach has been added. Height changes use existing physical-height calibration. The current single-hex footprint is retained.

Validation: focused AI/direction/center-pickup/material tests plus the full combat regressions.
