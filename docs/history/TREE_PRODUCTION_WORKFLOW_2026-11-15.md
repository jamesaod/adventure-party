# Tree production workflow

Working record — 15 November 2026. Keep local until publication is requested.

## 1. Define the tree
Record species, biome, mature height/crown width, lowest branch clearance, source ground level, tree spacing radius and grass feather. White oak: raised crown with at least 10 ft clearance requested; source height 25.473 m above the 0.20 m ground plane. Validate clearances against exported geometry, not only viewport appearance.

## 2. Preserve and author in SpeedTree
Keep the original SPM. Save each experiment under a new name. Preserve the seed, trunk and major limbs across seasonal versions. Author green for Spring/Summer, autumn foliage for Fall, bare branches for Winter. Keep fallen log, broken branches, stump and leaf-fall sources separately. Ask before computer use, per user preference.

Current sources: WhiteOak_RaisedCrown.spm, WhiteOak_Fall.spm, WhiteOak_Winter.spm, WhiteOak_Winter_FineBranches.spm, WhiteOak_Fallen.spm, WhiteOak_Stump.spm, WhiteOak_LeafFall.spm. Originals live in the development workspace outputs/white-oak folder.

## 3. Export and measure
Export Games Wavefront OBJ, metres, Z-up, scale 1, all LODs, no coordinate flips. Retain MTL and color/normal maps. Count triangles (not ambiguous polygons), bounds, material groups and texture dimensions. Preserve alpha cutouts. Exported counts can differ from the editor preview.

Current game: green 14,447 triangles; Fall 13,894; original Winter 9,036; fine Winter 35,349. Fine Winter adds a child generator to Twigs: count 4, length 22% of parent, radius 24%, segment scalar 0.12. This is an appearance test, not an approved forest-wide polygon budget. User is assessing its performance; preserve the lighter winter option and LODs.

## 4. Convert for the game
Convert OBJ Z-up to glTF Y-up, preserving normals and flipping V for glTF textures. Existing scale: 43.38933076913966 model units/metre, renderer scale 0.18. Subtract source ground 0.20 m before conversion; clip underground roots in body and depth shaders. Use unique seasonal material names and asset filenames; retain provenance and original assets. Do not normalize each season independently.

## 5. Integrate season and footprint
The shared season service maps Spring/Summer to green, Fall to autumn, Winter to bare/fine branches. Model indices distinguish geometry, silhouettes, bounds, snow and shadow caches. Season changes do not set weather or reset snow. White oak has two-hex axial tree exclusion with grass absent inside 1.35 hexes, feathered over the outer 0.65. Future tree size overrides belong in tree-environment-profiles.mjs. Preserve ground textures.

## 6. Ground litter
Extract leaf cards from the same SpeedTree tree and retain source UVs/textures. Current Fall scatter instances the two-triangle OakLitter_LeafCluster asset at varied rotations/sizes, with deterministic placement under the oak and a feathered edge. Scatter extends to 1.25 times its exclusion radius. Roots respect grass floor fragments, visibility, water rejection and ground elevation. GPU snow burial and illumination use the existing ground-cover pass. No leaves in other seasons; no new animation loop. Existing piles and dense/sparse assets remain available for later work.

## 7. Validate and approve
Check all four seasons and six map rotations, root burial, silhouette/color, branch clearance, water edges, snow burial, grass exclusion and cached layout reuse. Compare CPU frame submission, GPU ground cover/tree passes, instance/triangle counts and draw calls at the same camera and weather. Verify no shader/load errors. Give the user concise counts and known tradeoffs; visual approval remains with the user. Keep source/export/conversion scripts and backups so changes can be reproduced or reversed.

## Outstanding decisions
- Forest-scale winter LOD and polygon budget after user performance review.
- Season transitions, persistent leaf accumulation, wind-driven leaves and leaf-fall animation are not implemented.
- Spring currently shares Summer foliage. Fallen logs and stumps are authored assets awaiting gameplay integration.

Leaf refinement: ground litter uses rust-brown shading (RGB multiplier 0.62/0.48/0.36 with deterministic 0.78–1 brightness). oak-fall-particle-leaf.gltf is a reusable two-triangle, double-sided 14 cm leaf card sampling one isolated leaf from the same Fall atlas; crop/provenance are in glTF extras. Emitter/motion is not connected yet. Oak snow shells exclude source triangles entering the bottom 0.5 m, so buried root/cap atlas geometry cannot form raised snow lumps; ground snow remains responsible there.

Seasonal outline policy: Winter white oak has no silhouette outline, including during snow. Spring/Summer retain the normal outline; Fall retains its deep reddish-orange outline. Other tree species are unaffected.

Falling leaves: Fall oak emits a fixed GPU pool of 96 two-triangle cards (192 triangles). Authored foliage vertices provide deterministic release positions. Vertex-shader descent, flutter, tumbling, wind drift and fade use the existing environment frame scheduler. Other seasons, burnt/burning trees, shadow passes and silhouette masks do not emit leaves; reduced-motion disables the effect. Ground litter remains separate and does not grow without bound.

Appearance tuning: falling leaf cards scale 2.5x (nominal 35 cm, varied) to match ground-scatter card dimensions. Oak shelter uses the full species-aware tree size, 1.15x canopy radius, and a wider core with deterministic irregular feathering. This shared precipitation exposure field controls the existing snow shelter and contact transition; it is cached and uploaded only on field changes. Fir profiles remain unchanged.

Fall outline fades with smoothstep(daylight, 0, 0.65): zero at night, gradual dawn/dusk transition, full configured opacity in daylight. Fade applies to both colored and snowy outline edges; per-draw state resets preserve other tree outlines.

### Seasonal leaf atlas and ground handoff (2026-11-15)
Use the existing individual-leaf UV cut (the particle-card glTF's cropPixels provenance) with the existing autumn litter atlas and the matching Oak_English_Cluster_Leaf_Summer_Color atlas. UV extraction avoids resampling artwork or generating new SpeedTree sources. Summer/spring now sample the real green artwork rather than recoloring the autumn leaf. Ground litter selects the same seasonal atlas.
Each fall precomputes 6,560 deterministic landing records once per season and converts them into stable world-space deposits. Deposit arrival times are packed in the litter instance field and gated on the GPU; airborne cards disappear at their own landing time. Deposits use the existing litter geometry, local ground illumination, water exclusion and snow burial. They clear on reset. Spatial buckets limit placement checks to nearby floor fragments; the array identity remains stable while scrolling and during flight.
The airborne pool has its own tree-scene group and is drawn in actor depth intervals using shader clipping, with one shared effect time per frame. It is absent from tree-body and ground-shadow passes. Landed litter renders before all actors, eliminating the old tree-pass overlay on characters. Settled events issue no airborne draws. Reduced-motion handling still jumps to the final state. Hidden-browser checks passed summer/fall/winter, persistent deposits, reset, rotation, pool shutdown and cache stability; CPU tests verify floor litter is drawn before the first actor and existing pan cache invariants. User visual review remains authoritative for exact leaf color/scale and airborne overlap.

### Six-direction falls and debris navigation
Each FELL OAK request shuffles the six hex directions and selects a clear one. Existing standing orientation is preserved with a local yaw compensation; the trunk, branches, leaf flight, litter patch and shadows share the chosen world fall direction. A geometry-derived capsule is fitted to the principal connected wood of the trunk, stump and eight large limbs. Tiny detached twigs and leaves are excluded. Capsule geometry is cached per seasonal model/direction.

The incoming fall reserves a wider swept area immediately, then narrows to settled debris after six seconds, independently of camera visibility. Existing unit footprints are checked before a fall begins; active walking or no clear direction refuses the test with CLEAR FALL AREA rather than damaging or trapping a unit. The stump retains the original root obstacle. Movement destinations, AI path edges, rotating footprints and smoothed walks all respect debris; reachability caches distinguish reservation, settled and reset states. These obstacles do not alter visual LOS/cover. Reset removes the debris/reservation and restores the standing tree.

Validation: all six directions reachable in a clear test; occupied-area and walking guards; ten principal-piece capsules; reservation and settled blocking; cross-trunk edge rejection; fixed obstacles during camera rotation; clean reset. One measured direction reserved 16 hexes and settled to 8, with gaps remaining around the fitted pieces. Seasonal rendering, litter, muted sound playback/reset, destination movement, multi-hex footprints, doorway movement, Quick Command and resident grass-cache checks passed. Quick Command fixture received missing weapon/archer stubs so its existing movement-only test can execute.

Fallen-wood grass pressure: trunk/stump/large-branch capsules bake a 256×256 feathered pressure field only when the footprint identity changes. Original and canopy grass sample it on the GPU and use the existing blade-laying deformation; wind is reduced under pressure. Pressure ramps from impact (2.6 seconds) to full weight at four seconds, remains after settlement and clears on reset. Snow pressure combines by maximum, so neither effect raises compressed grass. No placement rebuilds or per-blade CPU work are added. Field/core/feather/reset/upload-cache tests, grass-pan regression and the full fall/navigation browser check pass.
