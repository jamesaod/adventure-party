Weather & soil

The full patch uses the game’s grass texture, soil patches, grass clumps, rocks and sticks. It starts dry. Turn up rainfall to compare the same field as it gets wet.

Accelerated weather: wetness and shallow water update at 10 Hz. Wetness and mud behavior are provisional. Footprints currently depress the ground and can hold water; these rules are the next design step. Water uses tinted highlights and ripples, without scene reflections. Foot contacts are approximate in this prototype.
