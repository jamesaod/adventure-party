What this test measures

This variant samples the authored Death02 and Crawl Exit poses into the published Orc rig. It tracks only an outward root trajectory and pose blending—no Rapier world, rigid bodies, joints, equipment debris or corpse bake are created. The presentation scheduler stops requesting frames when the scene is idle. The 1 / 4 / 8 run reports pose-update measurements from this device.

At the blast, the Orc root turns so its +Z-forward axis matches its travel direction. Death02 plays while its root follows a short outward arc, ending prone on the ground still facing away from the blast. Surviving Orcs then blend from that final Death02 pose into UAL1 Source Crawl Exit and Idle. Weapons and shields remain part of the authored animation in this low-cost comparison. Combat damage, legal hex destinations and turn timing are not connected. Death02 / Crawl Exit provenance.
