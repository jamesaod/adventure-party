Grass & scatter

Clear-noon battlefield lighting reference. Live game renderer. Adjust a sample patch, then export your choices. Changes here do not modify the game.

Height, width, spread, bend and brightness are multipliers of the current game settings. Density is tufts per accepted cell. Wheel to zoom. Worn patches share one soft mask for soil blending and grass thinning. Soil bump is shading only, derived from texture detail. Negative values invert the relief.
