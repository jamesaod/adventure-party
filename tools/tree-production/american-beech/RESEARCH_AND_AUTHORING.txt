AMERICAN BEECH — research and authoring brief (2026-11-15)
Species: Fagus grandifolia. One new catalog specimen; preserve existing oak and three maples. No selector replacing simultaneous comparison trees.

Botanical references reviewed:
https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/fagus-grandifolia
Typical 50–70 ft; spreading crown, smooth blue-gray bark. Alternate pointed leaves 5–13 cm long, 9–14 lateral vein pairs terminating at teeth. Distinguish from European beech.
https://www.pubs.ext.vt.edu/content/pubs_ext_vt_edu/en/HORT/HORT-6/HORT-6.html
Approximately 60 ft tall, oval/rounded crown, lime spring growth and gold-brown fall foliage. Tan winter leaf retention especially juvenile trees; do not give a mature tree a fully leafy winter crown automatically.
https://www.forestry.alabama.gov/Pages/Management/Forms/Forest_Trees_Alabama.pdf
Search excerpt: forest-grown form has taller clear trunk and compact crown; open-grown form has spreading limbs and slender somewhat drooping branches. Full PDF still to review.

SpeedTree sources reviewed:
https://docs.unity3d.com/speedtree-modeler/manual/branch-generator-properties.html
Branch gravity positive down / negative up. Use start angle and restrained gravity for spreading limbs and fine branch tips; preserve continuous trunk taper.
https://docs.unity3d.com/speedtree-modeler/manual/leaf-mesh-generator-properties.html
Actual size preserves authored mesh dimensions. Size scales unit meshes; weld anchors leaves onto parent skin. Sky influence, fold, align and face control orientation; interval/phyllotaxy support side-consistent orientation. Keep physical leaf size independent of crown density.
https://docs.unity3d.com/speedtree-modeler/manual/seasons.html
Season appearance combines viewport season, generator and material curves. Exports capture a single seasonal state, not continuous season animation; keep our matching seasonal siblings.
