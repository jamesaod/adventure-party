Processing
Recorded handling/draw excerpts and up to six separated takes per fast event recording. Original 192 kHz rate retained for Still North Media. Other sources retain complete takes and original rates; all audition clips are PCM16 WAV with 4 ms edge fades. Any MP3 over-range peaks receive only the attenuation recorded in gainDb. Exact original files, offsets and hashes retained.

The 3 SFXMint downloads are AI-generated. The qubodup impacts are general material effects. TinyWorlds clips are footstep Foley candidates, not arrow recordings. No sounds have been assigned to live gameplay.
The original recordings remain in originals/. All source and clip checksums and timestamps are in catalog.json and selected JSON exports.
