Processing
Male files are retained as individual takes. The female recording is split
into seven reactions at pauses. Leading and trailing silence is trimmed,
with about 100 ms padding where available and 4 ms fades at clip edges.
Sample rates, channel counts and recorded levels are preserved. No generated
voices, pitch changes or character assignments have been applied.
The original recordings are kept in originals/. Exact timestamps and SHA-256
checksums are recorded in catalog.json and included in the selection export.

This page is an audition tool. Choosing or exporting clips does not change
the game's sounds; the JSON records choices for later integration.
